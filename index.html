System & Environmental Notes
  • Peak Sun Hours (PSH): regional PSH values are derived from historical Philippine meteorological irradiation averages (Luzon ~4.5 hrs, Visayas ~4.8 hrs, Mindanao ~5.1 hrs).
  • Ambient thermal derating: panel performance deratings account for high ambient roof temperatures common in tropical environments, reducing efficiency relative to standard test conditions (STC).
  • Battery usable vs. nominal: required total battery capacity accounts for maximum depth of discharge (DoD) thresholds to preserve battery operational lifespan (80% for LiFePO4, 50% for lead-acid).
  • Grid CO₂ emissions offset: environmental calculations assume a Philippine grid emission factor of approximately 0.70 kg CO₂ per kWh avoided.
  • Grid tie & net metering: yield projections assume optimal load matching or net metering integration with local utility distributors for excess generation export.
